# iamllm brand

**Name:** iamllm

**Tagline:** I am the language model. / 我就是这个语言模型。

The mark combines three ideas:

- a speech bubble for standard chat interfaces;
- a lowercase “i” for the person behind each instance;
- a pulse line for the human who actually answers.

## Colors

- Pulse Ink — `#111A16`
- Warm White — `#F7F8F3`
- Live Lime — `#B9E83F`
- Supporting Green — `#55705F`

Keep clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of its width. Do not recolor the pulse red, add robot imagery, or place text inside the mark.
